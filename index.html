They Said - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

They Said ("the app") is a promise and commitment tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information You Enter

They Said allows you to enter information such as people's names, promise details, notes, dates, reminder preferences, and promise status. This information is used to provide the app's core functionality.

2. Local Storage

Your promise information is primarily stored locally on your device. The app does not require you to create an account or maintain an online profile to use its core functionality.

3. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, They Said may send reminder notifications related to promises you have saved in the app. You can control notification permissions through your device settings and the app's settings.

4. Shared Content

If you use Android's share feature to send content to They Said, the app may process the shared text to help you create a promise. Shared content is used only to provide the requested app functionality.

5. Data Sharing

They Said does not sell your personal information. The app does not provide your saved promise information to third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

They Said may offer optional subscriptions through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. They Said does not receive or store your full payment card details.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may use services provided by Google Play and Android to distribute the application, process purchases, deliver notifications, and provide platform functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your saved information using the deletion and reset features provided inside the app. Because the core promise data is stored locally, uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

9. Children's Privacy

They Said is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality or privacy practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
